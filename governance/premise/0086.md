Findings for RFC 0086 (byte-type-v2).

1. Trap kind `byte-out-of-range` costs 8 tokens; `byte-range` costs 4 (28 tokens over the RFC's 7 uses). Use the shortest unambiguous kind, in the kernel's trap form.
2. Names `Byte`, `byte`, `int` and having no literal are already minimal. Re-propose against the new kernel's syntax (no `;`, `put x`, new signature form).
