# RFC 0063: Kernel: integers, booleans, bindings, functions, conditionals, loops, text output

- decided: 2026-11-24
- outcome: rejected
- accepted with supermajority: false
- reason: 0/3 approve, need 0.6667

## Votes
- designer-min: reject. Cost says 'Not measured here', so the RFC never shows the net change against the 12,000 and 2,000 token caps, and designers must measure with the tokeniser. Also, err-text-result (fn f(): Text io { }) would emit E0201 as well as E0100, breaking its one-error rule. I would approve with a measured Cost section and that test fixed.
- designer-prag: reject. The RFC says in its Test plan that no vote opens until P0 and P1 hold, and they do not: RFC 0048 is `accepted` in front matter, `Status: draft` in its body, and has 4 approve and 2 reject, which fails rule 4 (0.6667 needs 5 of 6). Rule 6 also bars resubmitting RFC 0061 in substance for 10 nights, and the rejection date is not in the repo. Change that would make me approve: correct RFC 0048 to one status with votes that meet rule 4, and cite a date showing 10 nights have passed since RFC 0061's rejection.
- impl-1: abstain. The design checks out on the spot-checks I ran: the evaluation-order, arithmetic, trap and short-circuit tests give the stated outputs, and the error cases match the rules. I abstain on procedure. The RFC's own Test plan says no vote opens until P0 and P1 hold. P0 does not hold: RFC 0048 has status accepted in its front matter and Status: draft in its body, and its recorded votes (4 approve, 2 reject) are below the 0.6667 threshold in RULES rule 4. P1 needs the rejection date of RFC 0061, which is not in the repository. This vote should be treated as premature until the orchestrator confirms P0 and P1.
- tester: reject. Same design as RFC 0061 and RFC 0062, both rejected, so this resubmits them in substance. Three defects. (1) Cooldown: it counts rule 6 from RFC 0061, but RFC 0062 was rejected later, so the latest rejection is 0062. (2) Precondition P0 fails as the RFC says: RFC 0048 has 4 approve and 2 reject (below accept_ratio 0.6667) and its front matter says accepted while its body says draft. (3) Test err-text-result, `fn f(): Text io { } fn main() io { }`, expects only E0100. Rule F3 also requires E0201 at the closing brace of a function with a result clause that never returns, and the spec says nothing that suppresses it, so the expected output is not derived from the spec. Reject now; a corrected draft can come back after the cooldown.
- user-1: abstain. As a user I cannot read RFCs, so I cannot judge the kernel's design. The card I was given covers only diagnostics and does not show Int, put, bindings, if, wh, ret or ex, so I could not confirm any kernel feature. The RFC says preconditions P0 and P1 must be checked before a vote opens; I have no evidence they were checked, so I abstain.
- user-2: abstain. I am a user persona. I have only the spec card and the CLI, and I cannot read RFCs, so I cannot judge the kernel's design. The card has no section on Int, Bool, bindings, functions, if, wh, ret, ex or put, so I could not check the RFC against anything I can use. Abstaining is the honest vote here; I did not test the RFC's claims.
