- designer-safe: approve. The decision is right: a record keeps only code, span and fix, and the words stay in the catalogue, which fits principles 4 and 8. Counter check: card K1 with its header is 223 tokens (+33, as stated), a fix record is 33, a no-fix record is 17, and the explain line is 30. Revision notes: D4's fix test ('a meaning the author could not infer') does not say which codes get a fix, and D8 drops the 20-byte quote limit and the wording-change clause without naming them; the 509-token core file is not verified.
- impl-1: approve. One decision: a diagnostic record carries code, span and optional fix, and the words of a code live in spec/errors.json and are read with glyphc explain. This is the constitution's principle 4 (nothing a model does not need to repair the code) and principle 8. I ran the counter: the fix-present record is 33 tokens and the no-fix record is 17, matching the RFC. Its 72-token current-shape sample I could not reproduce; my reconstruction is 59, so the saving is about 26 tokens per fix record, not 39. The saving holds either way and the figure is a note, not a reason to reject. Note for revision: a model that must repair a code it does not recognise now needs a glyphc explain call to learn the meaning. The RFC's claim that meaning is not needed for repair should be stated as a tradeoff. The E0010 entry already meets D6, and the E0003 entry is left to the implementing PR as the RFC says. Owner ruling check (open question 1) cannot be verified from here and does not block the decision.
- impl-2: approve. The decision is one: a record carries code, span and an optional fix, and the words of a code move to spec/errors.json, read with glyphc explain. This follows principle 4 (a diagnostic carries nothing the model does not need to repair the code) and principle 8 (fewer tokens). Counted with the protected tokeniser: the fix record is 33 tokens (the RFC says 33), the no-fix record is 17 (says 17), and the explain line is 30 (says 30). The Cost figures for the old shape (72 and 43) I could not check, because the RFC does not give that sample. Notes for revision, not reasons to reject: (1) Open question 1 cites an owner ruling on message.ts that the RFC cannot check; the revision should cite the ruling text or drop the claim. (2) The explain command (D7) and the catalogue shape (D6) are a second part of the decision; they are needed for the removed words to stay reachable, so I accept them as one decision, but the revision should say so in one sentence. (3) The test plan should name the file that holds diag/v9-explain, since that gate runs outside the repository.
