# RFC 0104: Lexical structure and core syntax

- decided: 2026-11-26
- outcome: accepted
- accepted with supermajority: false
- reason: 6/6 approve, need 0.6667
- approve 6, reject 0, abstain 0

## Votes
- designer-min: approve. Net removal: drops `;`, `fn`, `:`, `//`, the `\n` escape and `()` on parameterless declarations, and adds only the `put` and `fail` keywords plus rule 4. Non-blocking: the claim that the grammar needs one token of lookahead holds only after left-factoring the IDENT-led statements (`IDENT = expr` against `IDENT ( args )`).
- designer-safe: approve. I traced the layout rules, grammar and the span of about 50 compile-error and format tests against the rules, and they agree (for example v6-err-ret-mid at 12 to 15 and v6-err-cmp-chain at 15 to 16). Open question 2 leaves put(e) as a second spelling, which breaks principle 1 and contradicts the Alternatives entry that rejects it; the owner should settle it, but kernel-v46 does not depend on the choice.
- impl-1: approve. I checked the layout rules, the grammar, and the expected spans and outputs in the test tables against the rules; they agree. Conditions for the owner, neither blocking implementation: (1) open question 2 leaves put e and put(e) as two canonical spellings of one statement, which breaks principle 1 and contradicts the Alternatives entry that rejects put(x); (2) the claim that the grammar needs one token of lookahead is false at stmt, where IDENT is followed by = or (.
- tester: approve. Tester pass over the test plan. I checked the byte offsets and expected codes of the run and compile-error tests against the rules and grammar, and they agree (for example v6-err-semi at 13-14, v6-fmt-missing-space at 13-13, v6-err-else-if at 22-24, v6-err-eof at 13). The 15 keywords and 17 punctuation tokens match the counts. Open questions 1 to 7 are owner decisions and do not block the grammar. Not checked: the token-count figures in Cost, and the retirement mechanism.
- user-1: approve. Removes the separator and comment forms, makes ret end a block and put take a bare argument, and fixes one syntax so a model has one way to write each statement. The E0002 change for raw line feeds matches RFC 0102's own diagnostic table. Nothing here blocks a program I tried to write.
- user-2: approve. Removes the separator and comment forms, fixes one syntax, and makes LF in text a content byte, which lowers token cost and ambiguity. Matches principles 1 and 8. The LF diagnostic change to E0002 fixes a conflict with RFC 0102's own table. I could not test the grammar: glyphc has no front end yet.
