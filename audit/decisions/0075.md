Approve if the protected tokeniser measures the new card at 244 or fewer, or if the draft names a removal of at least 3 tokens from the card.

The RFC's own P0 check shows RFC 0048 is not accepted under rule 4 (4 of 6 is 0.66667, below accept_ratio 0.6667), so this amendment has no accepted base, and the fail-mark rule in F7 rests on P2, which was never run. - impl-1: abstain. I checked the merits and found no defect: the worked values in the run tests (arithmetic, remainder, overflow traps), the fail-mark assignments in the test programs, and the expected error codes all agree with F7 and the error rules. Card is 244 measured plus 3 by hand, under the P3 limit of 250. I abstain because the RFC's own preconditions are unresolved: P0 (its base RFC 0048 is marked accepted in front matter but draft in the body, with 4 approve and 2 reject, which is below the 0.6667 accept ratio) and P1 (RULES rule 6 cooldown for resubmitting RFCs 0061 to 0073 is unverified). P2, the fail-mark error benchmark, was not run. These are for the orchestrator to settle before a vote; I did not want my vote to stand in for that check.
- tester: reject. Principle 8 requires measurement before a longer form is allowed, and the `fail` token is a longer form that the RFC adds on the strength of principle 3 alone. P2, the fail-mark benchmark, is not run, so the constitution's condition is unmet and the RFC conflicts with it. Two more preconditions also fail: P0 (RFC 0048, which this RFC amends, is not clearly accepted; 4 of 6 votes is below accept_ratio 0.6667) and P1 (the RFC resubmits RFCs 0061 to 0074 in substance, which RULES rule 6 bars for 10 nights, and the cooldown is unverified). I checked the test plan by hand against F7 and the grammar and found no contradictions, so the objection is to the preconditions and the unmeasured fail token, not to the test rows. The codes E0200 to E0403 that the RFC uses are not yet in spec/errors.json; the RFC says the first emitting PR adds them, so that is not a defect in this RFC.
